Getting married at Tirupati: TTD's Kalyana Vedika, kalyana mandapams and what is not allowed

No wedding happens inside the Srivari temple. What TTD offers instead is the most organised temple-wedding system in India: a free venue on the hill, a registrar in the building and a network of bookable halls.

What is not allowed

Private weddings are not performed inside the Sri Venkateswara temple or before the main deity. The Kalyanotsavam you can book as a seva is the deity's own wedding, performed for devotees to witness, not a devotee wedding. Families who want to be married "at Tirupati" use one of the TTD venues below, with darshan arranged around the muhurta.

The Kalyana Vedika on Tirumala

Since 2016 TTD has solemnised weddings free of charge at its Kalyana Vedika on Papavinasanam Road on the hill: 26,777 couples by the end of 2025. TTD provides the priest, the mangala vadyam, turmeric and kumkum, basic rooms at a nominal charge, and a Hindu Marriage Sub-Registrar inside the complex so the marriage is registered the same day; couples also receive darshan tokens and laddus (Hans India; The Federal). The rules are firm: both parties Hindu, bride 18 and groom 21, first marriages only with parents attending (TTD states that love marriages and second marriages are not conducted), an unmarried certificate from the MRO, and reporting six hours before the muhurta. Slots are booked online on the TTD portal and open about 90 days ahead. Paid weddings with your own purohits also happen on the hill through the Tirumala Purohit Sangham's mandapam.

TTD kalyana mandapams in town and beyond

TTD runs roughly 250 kalyana mandapams: about 184 to 210 in Andhra Pradesh, 65 to 74 in Telangana (including several in Hyderabad) and a handful in other states. All are booked online through the TTD seva portal: choose the state, district, hall and date, upload a photograph and ID, pay, and carry the receipt and SMS to the hall supervisor. Permitted events include weddings, receptions, engagements, upanayanam, namakaranam and Satyanarayana vratam; DJ music is not allowed (TTD). TTD's published service charge is tiered by town size and is clearly historic (the Visakhapatnam hall was raised from Rs 9,440 to Rs 50,000 a day), so we quote only after checking the live portal rate for the specific hall (TTD tariff page). In Tirupati town the larger halls near the Srinivasam and Vishnu Nivasam complexes handle the big weddings; in district towns the halls are frequently available at short notice, which TTD's own board noted in 2025 when it found its mandapams underused.

Eligibility and documents

  • Both parties Hindu; bride 18, groom 21; first marriage; parents attend
  • Aadhaar (or passport for NRIs) for the couple and parents; age proof
  • Unmarried certificate from the MRO, or an affidavit for NRIs; passport photographs; the invitation
  • For registration at the Vedika: age proof, residence proof, a wedding photograph, the TTD receipt, the invitation and three witnesses

How we plan it

A Tirupati wedding is usually two days. Guests arrive at Tirupati (airport at Renigunta, trains from Chennai and Bengaluru) and stay in TTD and private accommodation in town; the pre-wedding rituals and the reception happen in a TTD mandapam in town; the wedding itself is at the Kalyana Vedika on the hill or at a town mandapam with your own purohits, followed by darshan arranged through TTD for the families. Dress is traditional throughout; the hill has its own rules on timing and vehicles that we build into the convoy plan.

Questions

Can we get married inside Tirumala temple?

No. Weddings are at TTD's Kalyana Vedika on the hill or at TTD mandapams; the temple itself is for darshan and sevas.

Is the TTD wedding really free?

Yes, at the Kalyana Vedika for eligible Hindu couples: priest, music and samagri are provided. Rooms are charged nominally, and anything beyond the basic ceremony (your own purohits, feast, photography) is yours to arrange.

Can NRIs book a TTD wedding?

Yes, as Hindus of age with parents present; a passport replaces Aadhaar and a notarised single-status affidavit replaces the MRO certificate. We confirm the document list with TTD for your dates.

Can we have a second marriage at the Vedika?

No. TTD conducts first marriages only at the Vedika. For other circumstances we plan at a TTD mandapam in town or at another temple.
