Clarity & assurance

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

Clear, unambiguous details on governance, permissions, timelines and costs.

Can anyone get married inside Sri Virupaksha or Bhoga Nandeeshwara?

Weddings at these temples happen with the permission of the temple administration, and some areas fall under heritage rules too. Each temple has its own process, timings and conditions. We secure these permissions for you and confirm the current rules for your dates as the first step after booking.

How far in advance should we initiate the booking window?

Six to nine months is comfortable for Hampi and Nandi Hills. Permissions, auspicious dates and hotel inventory in small towns all compete. Shorter timelines are sometimes possible; we will tell you honestly on the first call.

How do you handle nuanced or cross-regional family sampradayas?

The discussion rounds exist for exactly this. We sit with both families, document every custom and its order, and brief the purohits. When the two families follow different traditions, we plan a combined sequence so both are honoured in full.

How is pricing structured? Are there hidden vendor markups?

Every wedding is quoted individually after the discussion rounds, line by line: temple fees, purohits and samagri, stay, transport, food, décor, our fee. After the wedding you receive a reconciled statement against that quote. Rituals are never shortened to make a number work.
