How it works

You book once. We take it from there.

Your part is a few hours of conversation spread over a few weeks. Ours is everything else. This is the sequence, with what happens at each step and what we need from you.

Day 0

Consultation call

A free 30-minute call. You tell us the temple (or that you're undecided), the tentative dates or the month, where guests are coming from and roughly how many. We tell you honestly whether the dates are realistic for that temple and what the broad shape of the cost will be.

Within a week

Booking

A booking fee reserves our calendar for your dates and starts the permission file. It is adjusted against the final invoice. From this point you have one phone number for everything.

  • We send a simple agreement covering scope, timeline and payment stages
  • We immediately open the temple permission process, since that is the slowest piece
Weeks 1 to 4

Discussion rounds: three to four sittings

In person in Bengaluru or on video for families elsewhere. We ask both families to attend, with the elders who know the customs. Each round has a purpose.

  • Round 1, the tradition. Your sampradaya, gotra, the sequence of rituals as your family does them, any custom that must not be missed, any that the two families do differently.
  • Round 2, the people. Guest list, who travels from where, elders' needs, who stays how many nights, dietary rules, the family's VIPs and who looks after whom.
  • Round 3, the look and the day. Attire, makeup, flowers, music, photography, the timetable around the muhurta given by your astrologer or ours.
  • Round 4 (if needed), sign-off. We walk you through the full written plan and the itemised quote. Nothing is booked beyond the temple until you approve this.
After sign-off

Permissions and bookings

We close out the temple permission and every related clearance, then lock hotels, transport, cooks, purohits, artists and photographers. You get a confirmation sheet listing each one.

  • Written temple permission shared with you as soon as it is issued
  • Hotel room list by family name, travel manifest by flight and train
  • Purohit briefing document with your family's sequence, so there are no surprises on the day
One month before

Final walkthrough

A last review: headcount changes, dietary updates, any custom an aunt remembered. We re-confirm every vendor and send the families a day-by-day schedule with timings, locations, dress for each ritual and the on-ground contact for each.

Wedding week

On the ground

Our team arrives before the first guest and leaves after the last. Hospitality desk at the hotel, a coordinator at the temple, a runner for the purohits, a vehicle on standby.

  • Pre-wedding rituals at the hotel or a mantapa, then the temple for the muhurta
  • Elders escorted on the steps, seated early, fed on time
  • Food, flowers and samagri checked before each ritual starts
After

Settlement and send-off

Return transfers, a reconciled statement of every bill against the quote, the photographs and film when ready, and the dakshina to the purohits done properly. Then we leave you to be married.

What we ask of you

A few hours and clear answers

  • Attend the discussion rounds with the people who know your customs
  • Give us the guest list early, even if it changes later
  • Choose a muhurta with your astrologer, or let us arrange one
  • Approve the written plan before we book beyond the temple
  • Arrive a few days before the wedding for the pre-wedding rituals
What you can count on

One number. No surprises.

  • A single point of contact from booking to send-off
  • Written temple permission, shared with you
  • An itemised quote, and a reconciled statement after
  • The full ritual sequence, never compressed
  • Our team physically present through the wedding days

Start with the consultation call

Thirty minutes, no obligation. If your dates and temple are realistic, we'll say so. If they aren't, we'll say that too and suggest what would work.
