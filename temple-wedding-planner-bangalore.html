What a temple wedding planner actually does

A venue planner books a hall and a decorator. A temple wedding planner's work is different in kind. The first job is permission: the application to the temple trust or endowment office, the heritage clearance where the temple sits in a protected zone, the police intimation for the convoy, all of it in writing and all of it before any money goes to hotels. The second is the ritual: purohits from your own sampradaya, Smartha, Madhwa, Sri Vaishnava, Lingayat, Tamil or Telugu, briefed with a document of your family's sequence; samagri sourced to the temple's own mandates; a mantapa for the pre-wedding rites when the temple permits only the main rite. The third is the town: rooms blocked by family in places with twenty good hotels rather than two hundred, cooks who know the regional feast, convoys timed to a 5 am muhurta, elders escorted on stone steps. We do all three, and you speak to one person. See what we handle.

Who we are the right planner for

Families for whom the Naandi, the Kashi Yatre, the Dhare, the Laja Homa and the Arundhati darshana are the wedding, not a prelude to the reception. Couples who live in Bengaluru or abroad and want a temple their grandparents would recognise, without running the logistics themselves. People who would rather spend more than skip a step. We are not the right planner for a quick registry-style ceremony with a temple as a backdrop, and we say so on the first call.

Questions

Do you only plan weddings in Karnataka?

No. Karnataka is home and most of our weddings are here, but we plan at temples across Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, and at Triyuginarayan in Uttarakhand for families who want the Shiva-Parvati wedding temple.

Can we meet you before booking?

Yes. The first consultation is a free 30-minute call or a meeting at our Indiranagar studio. The discussion rounds that follow a booking are in person for Bengaluru families and on video for everyone else.

Which temple near Bengaluru is best for guests flying in?

Bhoga Nandeeshwara at Nandi Hills, 25 km from Kempegowda Airport, with resort stays ten to twenty minutes from the temple. ISKCON Bengaluru is the practical city option with large halls and parking.

Do you arrange the marriage registration in Karnataka?

Yes. Karnataka registers through the Kaveri 2.0 portal with three witnesses; we prepare the documents during the planning rounds and book the Sub-Registrar appointment around the wedding.
