What we handle

Everything. That is the point.

You should not have to call a temple office, a hotel, a cook and a priest and hope they agree with each other. One booking, one plan, one person responsible. Here is what that covers.

01

Temple permissions and liaison

The part most families underestimate. We identify every authority with a say over your ceremony, file the applications, pay the fees and deposits, and keep the file moving until the written permission is in hand.

  • Temple trust or devasthanam application and approval
  • State endowment / Muzrai department clearances where required
  • Heritage-site conditions at protected monuments (hours, headcount, sound, décor)
  • Local police and panchayat intimation for large gatherings and vehicle movement
  • Temple seva bookings: archanas, abhishekas and special darshan for the families
02

Rituals, purohits and samagri

Purohits chosen for your community's sampradaya, briefed on both families' customs, and given time to explain each step to the couple. Every item of samagri sourced, checked and laid out before the muhurta.

  • Nischitartha / Nischayathartham (engagement) and Naandi
  • Kashi Yatre, Varapooja, Gauri pooja, Mangala Snana
  • Kanyadaana, Dhare Herdu, Mangalya Dharane, Saptapadi
  • Laja Homa, Arundhati darshana, Nagavalli, Grihapravesha
  • Community-specific customs: Kannada, Telugu, Tamil Iyer and Iyengar, Malayali, North Indian Vedic
  • Vedic chanting, nadaswaram and chenda melam, veda pandits for the homa
03

Stay

Pilgrim towns have limited good rooms, and they go fast around auspicious dates. We block early, allot by family, and make sure elders are closest to the temple.

  • Heritage hotels, resorts and guesthouses, blocked as a group
  • Room allotment by family, with ground-floor and accessible rooms for elders
  • Hospitality desk at the hotel with a team member on call
  • Welcome kits, itineraries and muhurta timings in each room
04

Travel and transfers

Guests arrive from several cities and two or three countries. We make the last leg feel organised.

  • Airport and railway station pickups, coaches for the group
  • Temple shuttles timed to the muhurta, not the other way round
  • Separate vehicles for the bridal party and the purohits with the samagri
  • Sightseeing day for guests who arrive early (Hampi, Nandi Hills, Mysuru)
05

Food

Pure vegetarian, cooked by traditional cooks who know the regional menu for a wedding, served on banana leaf. We plan around fasting days and the elders' needs.

  • Breakfast, lunch, tiffin and dinner for each day of the stay
  • Wedding feast with the dishes your community expects (obbattu, payasam, the right pickles, the right order of service)
  • Prasada and naivedya prepared under the purohit's guidance
  • Diabetic, low-salt and soft-food options for elders; packed meals for travel days
06

Makeup, attire and jewellery

Dawn muhurtas mean 3 a.m. call times. Our artists are used to it, and used to temple dress codes.

  • Bridal makeup and hair for every event, with traditional hairstyles and flower jewellery
  • Makeup for mothers, sisters and the groom's party
  • Coordination of silks, dhotis, angavastras and the madisar or panche where custom calls for it
  • Jewellery logistics and safekeeping across venues
07

Décor

Temples set strict limits on décor, and that is a good thing. Within those limits we work with flowers, leaves, lamps and cloth.

  • Mango-leaf toranas, banana stems, marigold and jasmine
  • Brass lamps, kolam and rangoli at the hotel and mantapa
  • Mantapa dressing where a separate mantapa is used for pre-wedding rituals
  • Nothing that the temple forbids. No plastic, no nails in stone, no sound beyond the limit
08

Photography and film

Photographers who know how to work inside a temple: no flash where it is not allowed, no climbing on steps, and still the frames you'll keep for fifty years.

  • Candid and traditional photography, cinematic film
  • Temple-permitted equipment only; permissions for cameras handled with the main application
  • Family portraits scheduled so elders are not kept waiting
09

Guests and the little things

Someone from our team is visible at all times, so your family can be guests at their own wedding.

  • Invitations and RSVP tracking, printed and digital
  • Return gifts, thamboolam bags, tambula for the purohits
  • Medical support on call, wheelchair assistance on temple steps
  • Return transfers and a settlement of every bill after the wedding
Our one rule

Rituals are never shortened to save money

We'll help you spend wisely on hotels, travel and décor. We will not suggest skipping a homa or compressing the sequence to "finish by lunch". If a ritual is in your sampradaya, it happens, and it happens properly. Families who want it that way are the families we work for.
