Reservation Queries

Can non-resident or NRI couples plan remotely?

Yes. Discussion rounds run on video at times that suit your time zone, documents move digitally, and you arrive a few days before the pre-wedding rituals.

What happens if our muhurta falls during temple festival days?

Festival days change access, timings and crowd rules. We check the temple's own calendar before confirming, and if there is a clash we propose adjacent dates with your astrologer.

Are sacred rituals ever shortened for schedules?

No. If a ritual is in your sampradaya, it is performed in full. We plan the schedule around the rituals, not the other way round.
