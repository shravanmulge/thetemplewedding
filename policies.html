Policies

Privacy, Terms and Temple Protocols

Privacy Sanctum

What you share through our forms, WhatsApp or calls (names, contact details, dates, gotra, nakshatra, family customs, guest lists) is used only to plan your wedding. We share it with the temple administration and the vendors working on your wedding, and with no one else. We do not sell or rent it. Ask us at any time to delete what we hold.

Enquiry forms on this site open WhatsApp or your email app with the message pre-filled; nothing is stored on the website itself.

Ritual Terms

  • A booking fee reserves our calendar for your dates and is adjusted against the final invoice. The written agreement we send with it states scope, timeline and payment stages.
  • Temple permissions are applied for after booking and confirmed in writing before hotels and vendors are committed. If a temple declines the dates, we propose alternatives before any further money is spent.
  • Rituals in your sampradaya are performed in full. We do not shorten sequences to fit a schedule or a budget line.
  • Quotes are itemised. After the wedding you receive a reconciled statement against the quote.
  • Cancellation and change terms are set out in the written agreement for each wedding.

Temple Protocols

Every temple sets its own rules on dress, photography, sound, décor, timings and who may enter which part of the premises. Heritage sites add conditions of their own. We brief every guest before the wedding, and we ask families to accept that these rules are not negotiable on the day. Typical expectations:

  • Traditional dress inside the temple: dhoti or panche for men, saree or traditional attire for women, as the temple specifies.
  • No flash, drones or tripods where the temple forbids them; photographers follow the temple's instructions.
  • Only fresh, biodegradable décor; nothing nailed, taped or tied to stone.
  • Sound within the temple's permitted limits and hours.
