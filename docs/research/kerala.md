# Kerala temples that host weddings (2024–2026)

Scope note: Kerala only. Kollur Mookambika (Karnataka) excluded. Where a fact comes from a third-party booking-guide site (gotirupati.com, prayanamam.com, guruvayurtemple.org, guruvayoorlive.in, guruvayur-info.pages.dev) rather than the devaswom/temple itself or a newspaper, it is flagged "(third-party)". The official Guruvayur Devaswom site (guruvayurdevaswom.in / .org) returned HTTP 503 on every fetch attempt during this research, so its live marriage-booking terms could not be read directly.

## Key Question 1: Guruvayur Sri Krishna Temple — booking process, fees, volumes, slots, rules, devaswom halls

### Takeaway
Guruvayur is by far Kerala's main "wedding temple": weddings (thalikettu only) happen on mandapams at the eastern nada, 4–5 am to about 12.45 pm, with evening slots since 2023; record days in Chingam 2026 saw 416–437 weddings in a morning. Booking is through the Guruvayur Devaswom (official portal guruvayurdevaswom.in/#/marriageBooking per third-party guides, plus counter and same-day "spot" bookings); fee figures (₹500–₹1,250 all-in) come only from third-party guides and conflict, and the Devaswom restricts the wedding party to 20–24 people at the mandapam.

### Cited Findings

**Volumes and dates (newspaper sources, 2024–2026)**
- On the first Sunday of Chingam, 23 Aug 2026, Guruvayur temple held a record 416 weddings in just over seven hours, from 4.10 am to 12.45 pm, with the temple closing briefly for Ushapooja (5.47–6.12 am) and Pantheeradi pooja; 442 had been scheduled "including spot bookings"; ceremonies ran on six mandapams, the first at the eastern entrance; previous record was 334 on 8 Sept 2024 — [Onmanorama, 23 Aug 2026](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2026/08/23/416-weddings-in-seven-hours-guruvayur-temple-sets-new-record.html)
- Deccan Chronicle reported the same day as 437 weddings, ceremonies from 4.10 am, ~137 in the first two hours, 60+/hour across six mandapams in the courtyard, tokens issued to each wedding group, party limited to 24 people, regular darshan restricted, 100+ police, ambulances/doctors on standby; Devaswom chairman A V Gopinath quoted — [Deccan Chronicle, 23 Aug 2026](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/kerala/guruvayur-temple-witnesses-record-number-of-weddings-on-single-day-1981486); the 416 vs 437 figures conflict (437 may include spot bookings during the day; not confirmed).
- Kerala Kaumudi later reported 420 weddings on the final Sunday of Chingam 2026, surpassing the 416 of 23 Aug — [Kerala Kaumudi](https://keralakaumudi.com/en/kerala/general/guruvayur-breaks-wedding-record-with-420-marriages-1802857)
- Pre-day planning (20 Aug 2026): 429 weddings booked for 23 Aug, 342 for 30 Aug, 328 for 13 Sept; weddings begin 4 am; six mandapams for thalikettu with extra staff and traditional music teams; wedding parties admitted to Melpathur Auditorium before going to the eastern-entrance mandapam; tokens collected at a counter at the new nadappanthal on the south side of the auditorium (rest facilities there); only 24 people incl. photographers near the mandapam; eastern entrance one-way, exit via southern entrance — [Kerala Kaumudi, 20 Aug 2026](https://keralakaumudi.com/en/kerala/general/guruvayur-temple-set-for-record-weddings-in-chingam-1794143)
- As of 12 Aug 2026: 377 bookings for 23 Aug, "highest number of wedding bookings recorded so far"; the temple has four permanent wedding mandapams and usually adds a temporary one when bookings exceed 200; a Chennai devotee is funding a new permanent mandapam (sculptor Sudhil Kumar, Kothachira) — [Kerala Kaumudi, Aug 2026](https://keralakaumudi.com/en/kerala/general/record-breaking-wedding-bookings-in-guruvayur-as-chingam-arrives-1790527)
- 8 Sept 2024: 354 weddings solemnised (363 booked, 9 no-shows), starting 4 am, on four permanent plus two temporary mandapams; parties take a token from a counter at a temporary pandal beside Pattarkulam (south side) and enter the mandapam on priority; only 24 incl. photographer allowed; photography not allowed on the kizhakke nadappanthal after the wedding; darshan near the deepasthambham restricted; parking at the east-side multi-storey complex and Devaswom Srikrishna HSS ground — [Kerala Kaumudi, Sept 2024](https://keralakaumudi.com/en/kerala/general/record-marriages-in-guruvayur-ambalanadayil-354-weddings-solemnized-1380258); Onmanorama put the 2024 figure at 356 — [Onmanorama, 8 Sept 2024](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2024/09/08/guruvayur-temple-hosts-356-weddings.html)
- Older benchmark: 264 weddings in a day (2017 Athachamayam day) — [The News Minute](https://thenewsminute.com/article/264-weddings-day-marrying-keralas-guruvayur-temple-faster-making-maggi-49390); a 2019 travel feature said "up to 110 marriages are held every day" on ordinary days — [Onmanorama 2019](https://onmanorama.com/travel/kerala/2019/01/28/parassinikadavu-muthappans-kannurs-deity.html) (the Guruvayur figure appeared in search context; treat as approximate).

**Timings / slots**
- Standard wedding window has been 5 am until noon (when the sanctum closes after the mid-day ritual); in Dec 2022 the Devaswom decided to also allow evening/night weddings after devotee demand, and the first evening wedding was held at 5 pm in April 2023; Devaswom provides neyvilakku, tulasi garland and nadaswaram — [Onmanorama, 28 Apr 2023](https://www.onmanorama.com/travel/travel-news/2023/04/28/guruvayur-temple-allows-evening-night-weddings.html). The article does not give the exact evening window or a separate fee.
- Third-party guide: ceremonies 5.00 am–1.00 pm — [guruvayurtemple.org (third-party, states it is NOT the official site)](https://guruvayurtemple.org/guruvayur-marriage-booking/)
- COVID-era (Dec 2020) procedure, now superseded but shows the standing mechanics: bookings at the Devaswom book stall in front of the temple; each group gets a token with a time slot; groups assemble at Melpathur auditorium (east gate), proceed to the mandapams in front of the eastern gate, pray at the deepasthambham, exit south; Devaswom provides photographers; Devaswom does not arrange the thali pooja — [Onmanorama, 2020](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2020/06/03/weddings-guruvayur-temple-june3-bookings-open.html)

**Booking channel**
- Official online marriage booking URL cited as https://guruvayurdevaswom.in/#/marriageBooking; terms (as reproduced by an unofficial guide): booker declares the devotees belong to the Hindu religion and only "bonafide Hindu devotees" may conduct a marriage at the Devaswom; bride min 18, groom min 21; ID proofs required at slot selection and originals plus printed receipt at reporting; maximum 20 persons (10 on the mandapam, 10 outside); receipt valid only for the selected date, "not a legal document" and not a marriage certificate — certificates must be obtained from local self-government bodies; Devaswom keeps no records for later retrieval; cancellation allowed up to one day before, no refund on devotee cancellation; Devaswom collects no extra service charge for online booking — [guruvayur-info.pages.dev (third-party)](https://guruvayur-info.pages.dev/marriage-booking)
- Third-party guide: marriage ticket + photography ticket; can be booked online via Devaswom website, at the temple counter, or on the wedding day at least 3 hours before; up to 30 days in advance; total ₹1,250 = ₹750 marriage service fee + ₹500 photo/video ticket + optional ₹100 thali pooja; two photographers and two videographers permitted; max 24 family members on the mandap; bride traditional saree, groom traditional dhoti mandatory (shirt allowed); footwear removed; legal registration separate, at Guruvayur Municipality with temple marriage certificate/receipt, Aadhaar, photos, DOB proof, two witnesses; newly-weds cannot enter for darshan immediately after the ceremony — [guruvayurtemple.org (third-party)](https://guruvayurtemple.org/guruvayur-marriage-booking/)
- Other third-party guides give conflicting figures: "Marriage enrollment Rs 500", "max 3 months advance online booking", "Thalikettu Rs 500 per couple", and one older guide says online booking is NOT available and bookings must be in person — [gotirupati.com (third-party)](https://gotirupati.com/?p=58666); [gotirupati.com (third-party)](https://gotirupati.com/?p=55212); [prayanamam.com (third-party)](https://prayanamam.com/?p=2117). These conflict with each other; none is official.
- "Spot bookings" on the day are explicitly mentioned as part of the 442 scheduled on 23 Aug 2026 — [Onmanorama](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2026/08/23/416-weddings-in-seven-hours-guruvayur-temple-sets-new-record.html)

**Decoration / special-arrangement rules (High Court, 2021)**
- In the Ravi Pillai son's wedding case (Sept 2021), the Kerala High Court took suo motu notice of cutouts and tree-branch decorations at the nadappanthal; the Devaswom administrator's affidavit said only "pushpalankaram" (flower decoration) had been permitted and extra decorations were removed; the bench said people may hold weddings at the temple but "it has to be restricted to the infrastructure available there"; the committee was asked to explain private security deployment and any blocking of devotees — [Onmanorama, 2021](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2021/09/15/ravi-pillai-son-wedding-guruvayur-kerala-high-court.amp.html)

**Entry, attire, non-Hindus (temple-wide, not wedding-specific)**
- Men: mundu, bare-chested; women: saree / set-mundu; salwar-kameez has since been permitted for women; non-Hindus not admitted — [Kerala Tourism FAQ](https://www.keralatourism.org/faq/is-there-any-dress-code-for-guruvayur-temple); [guruvayoorlive.in (third-party)](https://guruvayoorlive.in/guruvayoor-temple-dress-code); [hindu-blog 2007 (old)](https://www.hindu-blog.com/2007/07/dress-code-change-in-guruvayur-temple.html)
- Guruvayur has insisted on a certificate from Arya Samajam, Kozhikode, before admitting a non-Hindu (e.g., converts) — [Kerala Kaumudi](https://keralakaumudi.com/web-news/en/2020/05/NMAN0146632/1.html)
- 2018 decision to open the Devaswom Oottupura (dining hall) to non-Hindus was revoked/put on hold — [Onmanorama 2018](https://onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2018/04/25/non-hindus-guruvayur-temple-oottupura-decision.html); [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/kerala/guruvayur-temple-revokes-decision-allow-non-hindus-feast-hall-consult-god-80129)

**Devaswom-run halls/auditoriums (third-party tariff listings, undated/2024 copyright)**
- Poonthanam Auditorium (Guruvayur Satyagraha Smaraka Mandiram), fully centralised AC, rented for marriages and cultural programmes: full day ₹80,000; cultural programme 9 am–5 pm ₹40,000; 5–9 pm ₹25,000; Ambadi dining hall (5 pm to 3 pm next day) ₹15,000; Narayaneeyam dining hall ₹18,000; kitchen ₹7,000; 2-bed rooms ₹500 non-AC / ₹800 AC; phone 0487 2556537; rates "can be revised without prior notification" — [guruvayoorlive.in (third-party)](https://guruvayoorlive.in/guruvayoor-auditorium); same figures repeated at [gotirupati.com (third-party)](https://gotirupati.com/?p=62952) and [prayanamam.com (third-party)](https://prayanamam.com/?p=2212). Capacity reported as ~1,000 — [gotirupati.com (third-party)](https://gotirupati.com/?p=62952)
- Melpathur Auditorium (right of the East Gopuram; capacity 2,000+) is used as the wedding assembly hall and is rented only for programmes "offered to the Lord" (dance/kacheri ₹2,000 per 1.5 hrs; Kathakali ₹2,500; ganamela ₹3,000; bhajana ₹1,000), not for wedding receptions; Sreevalsam Annex AC conference hall ₹8,000 + tax (0487 2556535) — [guruvayoorlive.in (third-party)](https://guruvayoorlive.in/guruvayoor-auditorium)
- A third-party page says Guruvayur temple marriage halls (3 halls, AC and non-AC) rent for about ₹16,000–₹1,00,000, counter booking only with documents in person, "online booking not available for marriage halls", book 3–6 months ahead — [gotirupati.com (third-party)](https://gotirupati.com/?p=62949)
- Private halls in Guruvayur town (Kailasam, Pushpanjali, Rugmini, Town Hall, Rvees, Surabhi etc.) are NOT devaswom-run; a venue site says private kalyana mandapam rates start ~₹30,000 — [guruvayurtemple.org (third-party)](https://guruvayurtemple.org/guruvayur-marriage-booking/); [gotirupati.com (third-party)](https://gotirupati.com/?p=58113)

### Inferences
- The consistent newspaper-reported mechanics (token counter on the south side, assembly at Melpathur, 24-person cap at the mandapam, exit via the south nada, thalikettu-only ceremony of a few minutes) are the reliable core; fee figures should be presented as "approximately ₹500–₹1,250 per third-party guides, verify on the official portal".
- "Panchajanyam" and "Sree Krishna auditorium" do not appear in any source found; the devaswom's rentable wedding venue is Poonthanam Auditorium (with Ambadi/Narayaneeyam dining halls); Melpathur is for religious programmes and wedding assembly only.
- The 416/420/437 discrepancy likely reflects booked vs. actually solemnised vs. including spot bookings; cite 416 (Onmanorama/Kerala Kaumudi) as the solemnised count for 23 Aug 2026 and 420 for the later Sunday.

### Gaps
- Official fee schedule, slot structure, and advance-booking window from guruvayurdevaswom.in could not be read (site returned 503 repeatedly; the booking page is a JS single-page app).
- Exact evening/night wedding hours and whether they carry a different fee — not found.
- Whether a Guruvayur "Devaswom marriage certificate" is issued separately from the receipt — unofficial guides say only a receipt (not a legal document) is given; no official confirmation.

## Key Question 2: Other Kerala temples — where weddings are held, who administers, booking, rules

### Takeaway
Beyond Guruvayur, individual-wedding (thalikettu) practice is documented for Attukal Bhagavathy (Thiruvananthapuram, private trust) and Thiruvambady Sri Krishna (Thrissur, private devaswom with its own 1,100-seat convention centre), and TDB lists "Marriage" as an online-bookable vazhipadu across its temples; most other famous temples on the list (Chottanikkara, Ettumanoor, Vaikom, Thrikkakara, Kodungallur, Mannarasala, Padmanabhaswamy, Vadakkunnathan, etc.) have no published, verifiable wedding-booking information online, and a few temples are explicitly documented as not conducting weddings (Cheranallur Karthyayani — CDB; Kachamkurissi — MDB refuses "love marriages").

### Cited Findings

**Attukal Bhagavathy Temple, Thiruvananthapuram (Attukal Bhagavathy Temple Trust — private trust, not a Devaswom Board)**
- Weddings take place in a mantap inside the temple, last 10–15 minutes; bride 18+, groom 21+; "only Hindu arranged marriages" allowed; the temple receipt can be used for marriage registration; bride, groom and both sets of parents enter the mantap — [gotirupati.com (third-party)](https://gotirupati.com/?p=62966); [prayanamam.com (third-party)](https://prayanamam.com/?p=2217)
- Third-party guide adds: book by visiting/calling the temple office; both partners must be Hindu; documents = govt photo ID + SSLC for age; same-day darshan typically not allowed (next day OK); receptions must be at an external venue; traditional Kerala attire strongly recommended; photography/video allowed; no loud music; nearby private mandapams from ~₹30,000 — [prayanamam.com (third-party)](https://prayanamam.com/?p=2217)
- Official site attukal.org fetched but rendered no wedding content (JS page) — [attukal.org](https://www.attukal.org/). Fee not found.

**Thiruvambady Sri Krishna Temple, Thrissur (Thiruvambady Devaswom — private/independent devaswom, one of the two Thrissur Pooram devaswoms)**
- Official site lists "Marriage Halls": Thiruvambady Convention Centre / "Kousthubham", fully AC, 800-capacity auditorium (official page) plus a second smaller marriage hall; contacts: convention centre Marar Road, +91 487 2972442, thiruvambadyconventioncentre@gmail.com; marriage halls Shornur Road 0487 2334901; online vazhipadu booking at booking.thiruvambadytemple.com; no hall rent published — [thiruvambadytemple.com amenities](https://www.thiruvambadytemple.com/thiruvambady-devasowm-amenities.html); [thiruvambadytemple.com home](https://www.thiruvambadytemple.com/)
- Wikipedia: convention centre opened 29 Mar 2015, built and owned by Thiruvambady Devaswom, AC auditorium "Nandanam" seats 1,100+, dining hall "Dwaraka" 500+, Sreevalsam block with mini hall and 22 rooms, 1.25 acres, ~90,000 sq ft, parking ~140 cars — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvambady_Convention_Centre,_Thrissur) (capacity conflicts with the official "800" figure; the official site may refer to a different hall)
- Third-party: only thalikettu is permitted inside the temple; couple circles the lamp once and leaves; no darshan after the ceremony; photography/video allowed; bride 18 / groom 21; temple issues a receipt (does not register the marriage) — [prayanamam.com (third-party)](https://prayanamam.com/?p=2267)

**Thiruvairanikulam Mahadeva Temple, Ernakulam (Thiruvairanikulam Mahadeva Temple Trust — private trust)**
- Believed to grant the boon of marriage; the Trust organises an annual mass wedding ("Mangalyam 2026 Samooha Vivaham", 14 May 2026, seven brides) on the trust premises, Sreemoolanagaram; contact 0484 2600182 / 2601182; the page does not say whether individual weddings are hosted — [templesofkerala.org](https://templesofkerala.org/ernakulamfest/mangalyam.html); [pilgrimaide](https://www.pilgrimaide.com/temples/thiruvairanikulam-mahadeva-temple)

**Sabarimala (TDB)**
- Only Sabarimala-related bookable items are poojas/annadanam; no marriage item — [onlinetdb.com](https://www.onlinetdb.com/store.htm). (Sabarimala is a pilgrimage shrine with restricted season and no wedding practice; no source states this explicitly — see Gaps.)

### Inferences
- Outside Guruvayur, the institutions with a documented, organised individual-wedding service and own venue are private trusts/devaswoms (Attukal, Thiruvambady). Devaswom-Board temples generally treat "Vivaham/Thalikettu" as a vazhipadu booked at the temple office (and, for TDB, nominally online), not as a packaged service.
- A family should assume: (a) inside-complex ceremony = thalikettu only, minutes long, mornings; (b) devaswom-run hall = Guruvayur's Poonthanam and Thiruvambady's convention centre are the clear examples; (c) everywhere else, receptions/feasts are in private halls.

### Gaps
- No published wedding fee, slot, or hall tariff was found for Chottanikkara, Ettumanoor, Vaikom, Ambalappuzha, Thrikkakara, Kodungallur, Mannarasala, Haripad, Oachira, Kottarakkara, Sree Vallabha, Aranmula, Chengannur, Malayalappuzha, Kaviyoor, Thiruvanvandoor, Kalady, Thrikkariyoor, Peruvanam, Koodalmanikyam, Thiruvilwamala, Thiruvegappura, Mannarkkad, Kottiyoor, Thirunelli, Rajarajeshwara, Thrichambaram, Lokanarkavu, Paramekkavu. English-language web has essentially nothing; Malayalam sources or phone enquiry needed.
- No explicit source stating weddings are not held at Sabarimala, Padmanabhaswamy or Vadakkunnathan (absence of evidence only).
- Whether TDB's online "Marriage" vazhipadu is live at specific temples (Ettumanoor, Vaikom) could not be verified on the TDB portal.

## Key Question 3: Devaswom Board hall tariffs and online booking (TDB, CDB, MDB)

### Takeaway
None of the three statutory Boards (TDB, CDB, MDB) publishes a consolidated kalyana mandapam/auditorium rent list or online hall booking that this research could locate; only Guruvayur Devaswom (a separate statutory body) and the private Thiruvambady Devaswom have identifiable rentable halls, and even Guruvayur's tariff is available only via third-party reproductions.

### Cited Findings
- TDB online booking exists for pujas/tulabharam/marriage/choroon at temple level, but no hall booking — [Kerala Kaumudi](https://keralakaumudi.com/en/news/mobile/news.php?id=1297978&u=); [onlinetdb.com](https://www.onlinetdb.com/)
- CDB website shows tenders and temple groups, no hall tariffs — [cochindevaswomboard.org](https://cochindevaswomboard.org); [tenders page](https://cochindevaswomboard.org/tenders-old-7.html)
- Guruvayur Devaswom Poonthanam Auditorium tariff (third-party reproduction): ₹80,000 full day AC; dining halls ₹15,000–18,000; kitchen ₹7,000 — [guruvayoorlive.in (third-party)](https://guruvayoorlive.in/guruvayoor-auditorium)

## Key Question 4: Non-Hindu entry, attire, and legal registration of temple marriages

### Takeaway
Temple weddings in Kerala are restricted to Hindus (Guruvayur requires a Hindu declaration at booking; Attukal "both partners must be Hindu"), attire follows temple dress codes (mundu for men, saree/set-mundu for women; salwar now accepted at Guruvayur), and the temple issues only a receipt/solemnisation proof — legal registration must be done separately with the local body under the Kerala Hindu Marriage Registration Rules 1957 / Kerala Registration of Marriages (Common) Rules 2008.

### Cited Findings
- Guruvayur booking requires declaration that the parties are Hindu; only bona fide Hindu devotees may marry there; receipt is not a legal document; certificates from local self-government bodies — [guruvayur-info.pages.dev (third-party)](https://guruvayur-info.pages.dev/marriage-booking)
- Attukal: "Only Hindu arranged marriages are allowed"; temple receipt used for registration — [gotirupati.com (third-party)](https://gotirupati.com/?p=62966)
- Thiruvambady: temple does not register marriages; issues receipt for the registration application — [prayanamam.com (third-party)](https://prayanamam.com/?p=2267)
- Guruvayur insists on an Arya Samajam (Kozhikode) certificate to admit a non-Hindu — [Kerala Kaumudi](https://keralakaumudi.com/web-news/en/2020/05/NMAN0146632/1.html)
- Kerala HC: a self-declaration of conversion to Hinduism suffices for the registrar; registrar need not inquire into its validity — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/a-self-declaration-is-sufficient-for-conversion-to-hinduism-registrar-need-not-enquire-into-validity-of-conversion-for-marriage-kerala-hc)
- Kerala Hindu Marriage Registration Rules, 1957 (under s.8 HMA) govern registration — [LSGD Panchayat Wiki](https://panchayatwiki.lsgkerala.gov.in/KERALA_HINDU_MARRIAGE_REGISTRATION_RULES,_1957); application form — [india.gov.in](https://www.india.gov.in/application-form-registration-hindu-marriage-kerala?page=2)
- 2019 case: Guruvayur Municipality officials initially refused to register a Hindu couple's temple marriage because the bride's name "sounded Christian" — [Kerala Kaumudi 2019](https://keralakaumudi.com/web-news/en/2019/09/NMAN0090918/1.html) (page now returns HTTP 410; cite from search summary)
- Guruvayur dress code: men mundu, bare-chested; women saree/set-mundu, salwar-kameez permitted — [Kerala Tourism FAQ](https://www.keralatourism.org/faq/is-there-any-dress-code-for-guruvayur-temple); [guruvayoorlive.in (third-party)](https://guruvayoorlive.in/guruvayoor-temple-salwar)
- Padmanabhaswamy: Hindus only; churidar refused — [Onmanorama](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/churidar-clad-women-denied-entry-padmanabhaswamy-temple.html)
- Photography at Guruvayur: Devaswom photo/video ticket (₹500 per third-party guide), two photographers + two videographers; no photography on the eastern nadappanthal after the wedding — [guruvayurtemple.org (third-party)](https://guruvayurtemple.org/guruvayur-marriage-booking/); [Kerala Kaumudi 2024](https://keralakaumudi.com/en/kerala/general/record-marriages-in-guruvayur-ambalanadayil-354-weddings-solemnized-1380258)
- Sound/decor: only flower decoration permitted at the Guruvayur nadappanthal; extra lighting/sound at Melpathur needs separate permission — [Onmanorama 2021](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2021/09/15/ravi-pillai-son-wedding-guruvayur-kerala-high-court.amp.html); [guruvayoorlive.in (third-party)](https://guruvayoorlive.in/guruvayoor-auditorium)

### Inferences
- Inter-faith couples cannot marry inside Guruvayur/Attukal-type temples without the non-Hindu partner's conversion (Arya Samaj certificate route) and would otherwise use the Special Marriage Act.
- The "temple marriage certificate" families cite is in practice the devaswom receipt; registrars accept it as evidence of solemnisation along with witnesses.

### Gaps
- No official Devaswom circular text on dress code for wedding parties specifically (whether a groom may wear a shirt at the mandapam) was found; third-party guide says shirt allowed at Guruvayur.
- No official statement on sound systems/nadaswaram rules at the mandapams beyond the Devaswom itself stationing traditional music teams.
