# Karnataka temples that host weddings (2024–2026)

Scope note: Karnataka only. Research date 2026-10-30. Every fact carries an inline URL. Where only third-party/commercial sources exist, that is flagged. Several primary pages (Deccan Herald, Udayavani, TripAdvisor FAQ, eseva.kukke.org) returned HTTP 403 or did not resolve from this session, so some findings rest on search-result snippets and are marked "(snippet only)".

Venue-type legend used throughout:
- (a) = wedding inside the temple complex itself (sanctum area / temple mantapa / corridor)
- (b) = wedding in a kalyana mantapa / hall owned and run by the temple, matha or trust, adjacent or walkable
- (c) = only private halls nearby; temple does not host weddings

## Key Question 1: Sri Virupaksha Temple, Hampi — are weddings held inside? Who permits? Fees? Alternatives?

### Takeaway
Weddings are still conducted inside the Virupaksha temple (type a), but only in a stripped-down form: no feast, no photography, no cooking, no large gathering. Permission is routed through the temple's religious endowment (Muzrai) administration, which itself insists on archaeology-department clearance; ASI's Hampi Circle says wedding approval is "not within its purview". No published fee schedule or online process was found; a 2026 local report says weddings there have declined sharply because of these rules, and the district DC is reviewing the practice.

### Cited Findings
- Sri Virupaksha Temple and Complex is a nationally protected monument under the Ministry of Culture/ASI; ASI has previously accused the Karnataka endowment department of unauthorised works (metal pipes into pillars) in breach of the AMASR Act — shows dual control (Muzrai-run living temple inside an ASI monument) — [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/politics/asi-calls-out-karnataka-government-for-unauthorised-drilling-at-hampis-virupaksha-temple)
- "Strict Rules Lead to Decline in Hampi Temple Weddings" (21 April 2026): fewer marriages are held at Hampi's ancient temples because of rules from the central and state archaeology departments and the religious endowment department; restrictions on cooking and photography inside temple premises have pushed families to private halls; an endowment official said hundreds of weddings used to be held yearly but are now rare — [hospet.online](https://hospet.online/?p=18065)
- Same report: the endowment (Muzrai) department requires permission from the archaeology department before a temple wedding; ASI Hampi Circle superintendent Ramakrishna Reddy said approving/rejecting weddings is not ASI's purview, ASI "has never imposed wedding restrictions", only protects monuments, and has no objection to weddings near the temple if rules are followed; lack of coordination is blamed for confusion — [hospet.online](https://hospet.online/?p=18065)
- Same report: simple weddings without pomp still continue on temple premises, with priests performing rituals, provided there is no feast, no photography and no large gathering; cooking inside is not allowed; Vijayanagara DC Kavita S Mannikeri plans to review past practice and find a way to keep the tradition within departmental rules; no fees mentioned — [hospet.online](https://hospet.online/?p=18065)
- Example of an actual wedding inside the temple: 25 Nov 2022, a Vijayanagara man married a Belgian woman at Virupaksha temple at 9:25 am (Kumbha lagna); article does not mention who permitted it or the location within the complex — [ETV Bharat](https://etvbharat.com/english/bharat/belgian-bride-and-karnataka-groom-get-married-in-hampi-temple/na20221125175345501501261)
- Temple guides state the 38-pillared Ranga Mantapa is used for temple rituals including marriage ceremonies, and that villagers from nearby still prefer to marry within the premises (travel sources, not official) — [Pragyata](https://pragyata.com/splendours-of-hampi-virupaksha-temple/); [TravelTriangle](https://traveltriangle.com/karnataka-tourism/hampi/places-to-visit/virupaksha-temple)
- Photography/filming at Hampi monuments: Nov 2020 ASI Hampi Circle ordered an inquiry into an unauthorised pre-wedding shoot at Vittala complex/Kamal Mahal; ASI Dy. Superintendent M. Kalimuttu: "We have not given permission to shoot such videos or photographs"; complainant said shoots need approval from the Deputy Commissioner, SP, Hampi World Heritage Area Management Authority and ASI — [Daijiworld](https://www.daijiworld.com/news/newsDisplay.aspx?newsID=775316)
- Commercial planner Plannersy markets "Virupaksha Temple weddings", charging Rs 4,000 for "date & permission coordination" with "temple authorities and heritage administrators", and says the temple "follows strict protocols" as a UNESCO monument; photography must respect ASI and temple regulations; no capacity or official fee is stated, and the page's testimonial photos are actually of Srisailam — [Plannersy](https://plannersy.com/sacred-experiences/virupaksha-temple-wedding-planning/)
- Search snippet from a planner guide: permissions at Virupaksha are "rare and selective", approval depends on temple authorities and may need family references, guest count very limited, catering within premises not allowed (commercial source, snippet only) — [Plannersy blog](https://plannersy.com/blog/temple-wedding-in-bangalore/)

### Inferences
- Administering authority for weddings is the temple's endowment (Muzrai/HRCE) administration, which in practice asks for archaeology (ASI/State Archaeology) clearance; ASI formally disclaims a role. Families should expect to apply at the temple office in person; no portal or fee schedule exists publicly.
- The "wedding in Hampi" packages sold by planners are almost certainly these simple in-temple rituals plus feasts/receptions held in private halls or hotels in Hampi/Hosapete, since cooking and feasts are barred inside.
- No source mentions Hampi Bazaar mantapas or other ASI mantapas being used for private weddings; given ASI's event/photoshoot stance, assume they are not available.

### Gaps
- No official temple/Muzrai notice, fee, deposit, capacity or booking form for Virupaksha weddings was found online.
- Whether weddings are held in Hampi Bazaar / other mantapas: no source found (likely not permitted, but unconfirmed).
- Official ASI/HWHAMA written rule on ceremonies inside Hampi monuments: not found.

## Key Question 2: Bhoga Nandeeshwara Temple, Nandi village — Vasantha Mantapa weddings, booking office, fees, conditions

### Takeaway
The temple is an ASI-protected monument of national importance; weddings are performed in the stone kalyana/Vasantha mantapa (type a) with heavy restrictions (no photography inside, no fixings to stone, flowers/fabric only, food at the nearby Samudaya Bhavan). Sources conflict on current availability: a 2026 planner guide lists it as bookable through the temple office with 3–6 months' notice, while older third-party pages say weddings were stopped under ASI maintenance. Only an unofficial fee (Rs 1,000 enrolment, priest included) was found; no official booking page exists.

### Cited Findings
- The Vasantha Mantapa is a carved marriage altar in front of the Uma Maheshwari shrine, between the two main shrines; the temple is protected and managed as a monument of national importance by ASI — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhoganandishwara_Temple)
- Panigrahana (page reviewed 30 Aug 2026): ceremony is in the carved stone kalyana mantapa inside an ASI-protected complex; capacity is "mantapa-limited" so meal/reception usually moves to a nearby venue; booking through the temple office (no number given); fees not published; indicative notice 3–6 months on strong muhurthams; only flowers and fabric may be used and nothing may be fixed to the stone; Shivaratri (Feb) brings heavy crowds — [Panigrahana](https://panigrahana.com/temple-wedding-bangalore.html)
- GoTirupati (undated, third-party): "At present, marriages are not allowed as they are under ASI maintenance" yet the same page describes the mantap procedure: enrolment Rs 1,000 per couple with purohit included; garlands/pooja items extra; book at least 15 days ahead; ceremonies during temple hours, usually morning, 30–45 min; bride 18+/groom 21+; only Hindu arranged marriages, no love or inter-religion marriages; parents must attend; documents: Aadhaar, SSLC, 2 passport photos each, invitation, parents' ID, two witnesses; no photography inside the temple; food at Samudaya Bhavan or outside catering; "wedding without decoration" in the mantap; no marriage registration at temple — [GoTirupati](https://gotirupati.com/bhoga-nandeeshwara-temple-wedding/)
- Prayanamam (updated 17 Nov 2025, third-party): temple "currently under ASI maintenance", "prior authorisation is required"; no phone/office named; registration fee covers registration + priest; photography not permitted inside, shoots in the external hall; catering at Samudaya Bhavan outside the temple interior; extra decorations may need additional permits; book ≥15 days ahead — [Prayanamam](https://prayanamam.com/bhoga-nandeeshwara-temple-wedding-cost-procedure-online/)
- TripAdvisor FAQ (older forum answer, snippet only; page returned 403): procedure described as written permission from the ASI office in Koramangala (Bengaluru Circle) plus police and temple permissions, with conditions: no videography, no decoration, no chairs, no fire except ghee lamps — [TripAdvisor FAQ](https://www.tripadvisor.in/FAQ_Answers-g8153697-d3912850-t1366719-What_is_the_procecedure_facilities_and_charges.html)
- A photo-shoot vendor listing states the temple authorities have "closed the temple to weddings and pre-wedding shoots" (undated, snippet only) — [1Plus1 Studio](https://www.1plus1studio.com/blog/bhoga-nandeeshwara-temple-couple-shoot)
- Older first-hand accounts of weddings there exist (2018 travel blog; a photographer's wedding blog) — [Be On The Road, 2018](https://www.beontheroad.com/2018/08/bhoga-nandeeshwara-temple-traditional-wedding-destination.html); [Creative Chisel](https://creativechisel.com/blog/bhoga-nandeeshwara-temple-wedding/)

### Inferences
- The temple is under dual control (ASI monument; the living shrine's pooja administration is a state/Muzrai matter); practical approval involves the temple office plus ASI Bengaluru Circle, and a wedding there is a bare ritual with reception elsewhere.
- Availability seems to fluctuate with ASI conservation work; the "not allowed" statements likely refer to specific periods, and the 2026 guide suggests weddings resumed. Treat as "possible but confirm with temple office and ASI".

### Gaps
- No official booking office phone, fee schedule, or ASI permission form found; capacity figure not found.
- No news report (Deccan Herald/The Hindu) found confirming or denying a current ban.

## Key Question 3: Other Karnataka temples known for weddings — per-temple status

### Takeaway
The strongest documented wedding venues are the private-trust/matha-run pilgrimage centres (Dharmasthala, Kateel, Kukke, ISKCON Bengaluru, Sringeri), which run their own halls (type b) with in-person enrolment, age/eligibility rules and modest fees. Muzrai temples around Bengaluru (Banashankari, Kote Venkataramana) allow small in-temple weddings via the temple office (type a) with no published fees. ASI-protected Hoysala sites (Belur, Halebidu, Somnathpur) have no evidence of hosting weddings.

## Key Question 4: Does Karnataka Muzrai/HRCE publish rules or fee schedules for temple kalyana mantapas? Online booking?

### Takeaway
No published statewide Muzrai rule book or fee schedule for temple kalyana mantapas was found. Fees are set temple-by-temple by management committees; the state's ITMS/"Namma Muzrai" portals cover sevas and rooms, and early coverage said weddings would be included, but no live kalyana-mantapa booking on the state portal was confirmed. The state's own Muzrai-temple wedding programme is the "Saptapadi" free mass-marriage scheme at 'A'-grade temples, which has struggled with funds.

### Cited Findings
- The ITMS portal root page (itms.kar.nic.in) returned only an "HR-CE" heading with no service listing when fetched 2026-10-30 — [ITMS](https://itms.kar.nic.in/)
- Udayavani: new state app "Namma Muzrai" lets devotees book pujas and services; began as a pilot live only at Banashankari temple, Bengaluru (Minister Ramalinga Reddy) — [Udayavani](https://udayavani.com/english-news/soon-devotees-would-be-able-to-book-services-in-major-karnataka-temples-through-govt-app)
- Deccan Herald: app-based seva booking to roll out in 52 Muzrai temples; later software covers all 34,563 Muzrai institutions and online booking of temple boarding facilities — [DH 1](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/app-based-online-seva-booking-launched-at-select-temples-842158.html); [DH 2](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/karnataka-launches-new-software-for-muzrai-temples-1084451.html); [DH 3](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/pilgrimage-to-get-a-digital-spin-with-online-room-booking-in-karnataka-2696316)
- Hans India (older): Muzrai online services were to encompass special pujas, accommodation, "weddings and other auspicious ceremonies" — intention, not confirmed live — [Hans India](https://assets.thehansindia.com/news/cities/bengaluru/mujarai-department-to-launch-online-scheme-for-hassle-free-temple-services-818712)
- Service-fee revisions at 9 Muzrai temples from 1 Oct (2025): 5–10% hikes decided by temple management committees, not the state, after Agama-scholar verification — shows fees are committee-set per temple — [Daijiworld](https://daijiworld.com/news/newsDisplay?newsID=1292796)
- Saptapadi scheme: launched Jan 2020 by CM Yediyurappa for 100 'A'-grade Muzrai temples; benefits worth Rs 55,000 incl. 8 g gold thali, Rs 10,000 to bride, Rs 5,000 to groom; registration at temples 30 days ahead (later 15 days via taluk offices); objections window and final list — [Business Standard/ANI](https://business-standard.com/article/news-ani/cm-yediyurappa-launches-mass-marriage-program-in-bengaluru-120011000923_1.html); [Udayavani](https://www.udayavani.com/english-news/state-govt-announces-dates-for-saptapadhi-scheme); [Hans India](https://assets.thehansindia.com/karnataka/mass-marriage-scheme-saptapadi-in-all-temples-minister-kota-srinivas-poojary-678915)
- Scheme problems: only 10 couples married since launch at one point due to expenditure, funds crunch and staff shortage; "temples tied up in knots" (DH, 403 — headline only); government "to restart" scheme — [DH restart](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/karnataka-government-to-restart-mass-marriage-scheme-1100516.html); [DH knots](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fkarnataka%2Ftemples-tied-up-in-knots-with-saptapadi-free-mass-marriage-scheme-3569973)
- Six temples in undivided Dakshina Kannada were identified for Muzrai mass marriages (headline; page 403) — [Udayavani](https://www.udayavani.com/english-news/6-temples-from-undivided-dk-identified-for-mass-marriages)
- Karnataka HRICE (Amendment) Bill 2024 passed both Houses 29 Feb 2024 but was returned by the Governor seeking clarification — status unresolved in sources — [Deccan Herald tag](https://www.deccanherald.com/tag/muzrai-department)

### Inferences
- Kalyana mantapa charges at Muzrai temples are fixed in each temple's management-committee-approved seva/fee list rather than a statewide schedule; families must get the list from the temple office.
- Online kalyana mantapa booking: confirmed only at private institutions via email/phone (ISKCON); no temple in Karnataka was found with a true self-service online hall-booking page.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve any HRCE/ITMS page listing kalyana mantapa fees; could not verify a hrce.karnataka.gov.in URL.
- Current (2025–26) status of Saptapadi and the list of participating temples not confirmed (key DH/Udayavani pages blocked).
