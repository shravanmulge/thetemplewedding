Why a temple

A mantapa under lights is a venue. A temple is a witness.

Temples like Virupaksha and Bhoga Nandeeshwara have hosted weddings for hundreds of years. The deity is present. The purohits know the sampradaya of the place. The stone pillars have heard the same mantras you will hear. There is no substitute for that, and families who care about tradition know it.

What stops most people is the work. Permissions from temple trusts and government bodies. Priests who perform the full ritual and not a summary. Stay for two hundred guests in a small heritage town. Sattvic food cooked the way the elders expect. Transport, timings, muhurta, the hundred small things.

That work is what we do. You decide what matters to you. We make it happen.

What's included

Everything between "yes" and "married"

One booking covers the whole wedding. You never deal with a vendor, an office or a priest directly unless you want to.

Temple permissions

We apply to the temple trust, the endowment department and, where it applies, the heritage authority. We handle the paperwork, fees, deposits and the follow-ups that take months if you do them from another city.

Rituals and purohits

Purohits versed in your community's sampradaya, Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, Malayali or North Indian Vedic. Every homa, every samskara from Nischitartha to Grihapravesha, with all samagri sourced and laid out.

Stay for everyone

Heritage hotels, resorts and guesthouses near the temple, blocked for your dates and allotted by family. Elders close to the temple, cousins where the fun is.

Travel and transfers

Coaches and cars from the airport or railhead, temple-to-hotel shuttles timed to the muhurta, and a travel desk your guests can call.

Food, the traditional way

Pure vegetarian, sattvic, served on banana leaf by cooks who know the regional menu. Breakfast tiffins to the wedding feast, with elders' dietary needs and fasting days planned in.

Makeup, attire and décor

Bridal and family makeup artists who work at dawn muhurtas. Silk, jewellery and flower-jewellery coordination. Mango toranas, marigold, jasmine and banana stem décor that the temple allows.

Questions

Things families ask us first

Can anyone get married inside Virupaksha Temple or Bhoga Nandeeshwara Temple?

Weddings at these temples happen with the permission of the temple administration, and some areas fall under heritage rules too. Each temple has its own process, timings and conditions. We secure these permissions for you. Our first job after booking is to confirm the current rules for your temple and dates before anything else is committed.

How far in advance should we book?

Six to nine months is comfortable for Hampi and Nandi Hills, because permissions, auspicious dates and hotel inventory in small towns all compete. We have delivered weddings on shorter notice; ask us and we'll be honest about what's possible for your dates.

Do you follow our family's specific customs?

Yes. The discussion rounds exist for this. We sit with both families, note every custom and the order they're done in, and brief the purohits accordingly. If the two families follow different traditions, we plan the sequence so both are honoured.

What does it cost?

Every wedding is priced individually after the discussion rounds, because the temple, guest count, days of stay and the rituals chosen all change the number. We give you an itemised quote before you commit. What we won't do is shorten a ritual to make a number work.

We live abroad. Can we plan remotely?

Most of our families do. The discussion rounds happen on video, documents are shared digitally, and we handle everything on the ground. You arrive a few days early for the pre-wedding rituals.
