Things Families Ask Us First

Can anyone get married inside Virupaksha Temple or Bhoga Nandeeshwara Temple?

Weddings at these temples happen with the permission of the temple administration, and some areas fall under heritage rules too. Each temple has its own process, timings and conditions. We secure these permissions for you. Our first job after booking is to confirm the current rules for your temple and dates before anything else is committed.

How far in advance should our family book?

Six to nine months is comfortable for Hampi and Nandi Hills, because permissions, auspicious dates and hotel inventory in small towns all compete. We have delivered weddings on shorter notice; ask and we'll be honest about what is possible for your dates.

Do you follow our family's specific customs and sub-sect rituals?

Yes. The discussion rounds exist for this. We sit with both families, note every custom and the order they are done in, and brief the purohits accordingly. If the two families follow different traditions, we plan the sequence so both are honoured.

What does a complete temple wedding cost?

Every wedding is priced individually after the discussion rounds, because the temple, guest count, days of stay and rituals chosen all change the number. You receive an itemised quote before you commit. What we will not do is shorten a ritual to make a number work.

We live abroad in the US / UK / Singapore. Can we plan entirely remotely?

Most of our families do. The discussion rounds happen on video, documents are shared digitally, and we handle everything on the ground. You arrive a few days early for the pre-wedding rituals.
