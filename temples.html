Sri Virupaksha Temple, Hampi

The unbroken spiritual heartbeat of the Vijayanagara Empire along the sacred banks of the Tungabhadra. Worship has continued here for well over a thousand years, and the temple's own annual festival celebrates the wedding of Virupaksha and Pampa. Couples marry in the pillared courtyards where kings once knelt.

Bhoga Nandeeshwara Temple, Nandi Hills

One of Karnataka's oldest and most magnificent living jewels. The central Vasantha Mantapa was carved specifically for the divine wedding of Shiva and Parvati. The colossal stone-stepped kalyani lit with earthen ghee diyas creates an incomparable sacred aura, about 45 minutes from Kempegowda Airport.

Sri Cheluvanarayana Temple, Melukote

A serene hilltop sacred town sanctified by Sri Ramanuja, who lived here for over a decade. Weddings are celebrated in the heritage kalyana mantapas around the temple, with the town's famed puliyogare and sweet pongal prasada prepared by the hereditary cooks.

Meenakshi Amman Temple, Madurai

The Chithirai Thirukalyanam of Meenakshi and Sundareswarar is this temple's greatest festival, so a wedding here sits inside a living tradition of divine marriage. Tamil families marry in the temple's designated spaces with its own priests, under towering painted gopurams.

How Temple Permissions Actually Work

Navigating ancient trust charters, endowment departments and archaeological restrictions is complex. We demystify the four-step pipeline.

01

Temple Trust Consent

We formally submit your family's gotra, nakshatra and muhurta petition directly to the presiding managing trustee or dharmadhikari for preliminary blessing.

Step 1: 5 to 7 days
02

Muzrai & Endowment Clearances

Filing state religious endowment papers (Karnataka Muzrai, TN HR&CE, or Kerala Devaswom Boards) to deposit required fees and reserve the sacred mantapa.

Step 2: 14 to 21 days
03

ASI Monument Guidelines

For centrally protected monuments like Hampi, we secure Archaeological Survey of India compliance: no drilling, no open synthetic flames, strict eco-rules.

Step 3: Protocol audit
04

Advance Booking Windows

Securing auspicious muhurta slots 6 to 9 months ahead. We finalise room allotments, purohit rosters and logistics long before dates open to the broader public.

Step 4: Muhurta locked
