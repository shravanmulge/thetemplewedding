Temples

Living temples. Not venues.

Every temple below is a place of daily worship with its own administration, customs and rules for weddings. We know those rules, we hold the relationships, and we secure the permission in your name. If your family's temple is not listed, that is only because the list is short. Ask.

A note on permissions. Temple administrations, state endowment departments and, at heritage sites, the Archaeological Survey of India each have their own conditions on where ceremonies may be held, at what hours, with how many people and with what décor and sound. These change. We confirm the current rules for your temple and your dates as the first step after booking, before any money is committed to hotels or vendors.
Flagship · Karnataka

Sri Virupaksha Temple, Hampi

Deity: Virupaksha (Shiva) with Pampa Devi · District: Vijayanagara · Nearest city: Hosapete (13 km) · Nearest airport: Vidyanagar/Jindal (approx. 40 km), Hubballi (approx. 160 km), Bengaluru (approx. 340 km)

The oldest living shrine in the ruins of the Vijayanagara capital, on the southern bank of the Tungabhadra. Worship here predates the empire and has never stopped. The temple's own annual festival celebrates the wedding of Virupaksha and Pampa, which tells you how a wedding sits in this place.

A wedding at Hampi is also a wedding in a UNESCO World Heritage area, surrounded by boulder hills, the Hampi Bazaar and the river. Guests who arrive a day early get the coracle rides and the sunset from Hemakuta.

  • Ceremony inside the temple premises, subject to the temple administration's current conditions
  • Stay in Hosapete, Kamalapura and the resorts along the river; we block rooms as a group
  • Best season: October to February. Summers are hot; we do not recommend April and May for elders
  • Pre-wedding rituals can be held at your hotel or at a mantapa nearby, then the main muhurta at the temple

Flagship · Karnataka

Bhoga Nandeeshwara Temple, Nandi Hills

Deity: Bhoga Nandeeshwara (Shiva), with Arunachaleshwara and Uma Maheshwara shrines · Village: Nandi, Chikkaballapur district · From Bengaluru airport: approx. 25 km · From Bengaluru city: approx. 60 km

A temple complex more than a thousand years old at the foot of Nandi Hills, built up in turn by the Banas, Gangas, Cholas, Hoysalas and Vijayanagara kings. Its Vasantha Mantapa is a wedding hall by design: ornate black-stone pillars and a canopy carved for the celestial marriage of Shiva and Parvati.

The stepped tank behind the temple, the Shringeri Teertha, lit with oil lamps in the evening, is the setting families remember. The closeness to Bengaluru's airport makes it the practical choice when guests fly in from across India or abroad.

  • Wedding in the Vasantha Mantapa, subject to the temple administration's permission and calendar
  • Stay at resorts around Nandi Hills and Devanahalli, with room for a large party close by
  • Good year-round; the hills keep it cool even in summer, with the monsoon months the greenest
  • Easy to combine with pre-wedding events in Bengaluru the day before
