A Guruvayur temple wedding: how the thalikettu works and what a planner adds

Guruvayur is the busiest wedding temple in India and the simplest ceremony: a thali tied before Guruvayurappan in minutes. The art is in everything around those minutes.

The ceremony

A Guruvayur wedding is the thalikettu: the groom ties the thali before the deity on one of the four permanent mandapams at the eastern nada (temporary ones are added on peak days), followed by the pudavakodukkal and a circuit of the mandapam. The temple handles hundreds of these a day: a record 416 weddings in seven hours on 23 August 2026, with tokens issued at the south-side counter, couples assembling at the Melpathur Auditorium, a cap of about 24 people including photographers at the mandapam, and exit through the south nada (Onmanorama; Kerala Kaumudi). The Kerala High Court has affirmed that the right to marry at Guruvayur is "limited to the infrastructure available", with flower decoration only (Onmanorama).

Booking and timings

Weddings are booked through the Guruvayur Devaswom, a statutory body: online marriage booking on the Devaswom site, at the counter, or as a same-day "spot booking" if the day is not full. The booking requires a declaration that both parties are Hindu, bride 18 and groom 21, ID proof, and acceptance that the receipt is valid only for the chosen date and is not a legal document. Slots run from about 4 to 5 am until 12.45 pm, and the Devaswom has allowed evening slots since 2023. Chingam (August to September) and the Thulam to Makaram season are the crush; book weeks ahead for those. The temple's own fee is a few hundred rupees plus a separate photo and video ticket, which we confirm on the Devaswom portal at booking time rather than quote from blogs.

Rules: entry, dress, photography

  • Hindus only inside the temple, including guests. The Devaswom has asked converts for an Arya Samaj certificate. This matters for mixed guest lists; see our overseas guide.
  • Dress: mundu for men (shirts are now permitted), saree or set-mundu for women, salwar now allowed. No trousers, no Western wear.
  • Photography: a ticketed crew of two photographers and two videographers at the mandapam; no photography on the eastern nadappanthal after the ceremony; no drones.
  • No darshan immediately after the ceremony; the couple exits via the south nada and queues separately for darshan.

Around the ceremony: auditorium, sadya, stay

The Devaswom's rentable venue is the Poonthanam Auditorium (about 1,000 capacity with dining halls and a kitchen), booked at the Devaswom office; the Melpathur Auditorium is reserved for programmes offered to the deity and for wedding assembly. Many families instead take a hall or hotel in Guruvayur or Thrissur for the sadya, the Kerala feast on banana leaf, which we have cooked by traditional sadya teams. Stays are in Guruvayur's hotels and Devaswom guest houses, with Thrissur 30 km away for larger parties; the nearest airport is Kochi, about 80 km. Because the ceremony is at dawn, we move guests to Guruvayur the evening before and run a pre-dawn convoy.

Registration

The temple receipt is not a marriage certificate. Kerala's Registration of Marriages (Common) Rules 2008 require a memorandum within 45 days with the Devaswom's certificate and two witnesses; Guruvayur Municipality has a registration centre for this. We book the appointment as part of the wedding plan.

Questions

How long does a Guruvayur wedding take?

The ceremony at the mandapam takes about ten minutes; the token, assembly and queue around it take one to two hours on a busy day. We plan the morning around the token time, not the muhurta alone.

Can we have a full Vedic ceremony with homa at Guruvayur?

Not inside the temple; Guruvayur performs the thalikettu only. Families who want the full sequence hold the Vedic rites and homa at the hall or hotel the day before or after, and the thali at the temple at the muhurta.

Can our non-Hindu friends attend?

Not inside the temple. They join at the sadya and the reception. If that is a problem for your guest list, we suggest other temples where guests of any faith may enter.

Is Guruvayur suitable for elderly relatives?

With planning, yes: the walk from the nadappanthal to the mandapam is short and level, the crowd management is the challenge. We arrange wheelchairs, early positioning and a seated area at the auditorium.
